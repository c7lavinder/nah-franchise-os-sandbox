# North Star Metrics

## The Two Numbers That Run the Company

### Franchisees Active
- **Current**: 56 (from territories table, status = active)
- **Goal**: 250
- **Gap**: 194 territories to fill

### 10+ Houses Per Year
- **Current**: ? (from territory_profile.houses_purchased_ytd >= 10)
- **Goal**: 100
- **Valuation target**: 100 franchisees × 10+ houses/year = $100M+ valuation

## Primary Metric Per Territory
**Houses purchased in trailing 12 months** — this is THE number.

When a territory is below 10 houses/year:
- That is the coaching priority
- Runway pipeline tracks progress to first 10
- Running stage flags underperformers
- John's quarterly grades reflect this directly

## How Scout Uses These Metrics
- BI queries: always anchor to north star numbers
- Territory context: include houses/year in every territory mention
- Proactive flags: alert when territory drops below target
- Coaching: reference 10-house target in all operational feedback
